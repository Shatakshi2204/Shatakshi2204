# 💫 About Me:
I'm a data analyst who is enthusiastic about transforming data into insights that enable more intelligent financial and strategic choices. I have a keen interest in business analytics and finance. I like working with numbers, seeing trends, and decomposing difficult issues into understandable, useful solutions.<br><br>As a hobby, I work part-time in design in addition to analytics. I can express myself and remain creative thanks to design, which also enhances my ability to convey ideas visually. Good analysis, in my opinion, is about more than just numbers; it's also about how well those insights are communicated.<br><br>Analyzing minute details in commonplace objects is something I like to do in my spare time because it naturally stimulates my logical side. This practice keeps me inquisitive, perceptive, and lifelong learning while fortifying my problem-solving mindset.
